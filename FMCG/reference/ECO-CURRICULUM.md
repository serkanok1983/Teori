## A. Mathematical prerequisites
Assumed at the start: school algebra, graphs of functions, percentages, logarithms. Everything else is introduced just-in-time in Unit 2 (ECO-2.1–2.5) with applied statements only; proofs live in Euler's pure-math track (/home/box/math-curriculum/plan.md) and are cited by stage, never duplicated:
- Derivatives, optimisation in one variable → Euler Stage 5 (Calculus with proofs).
- Partial derivatives, multivariable optimisation, implicit function theorem → Euler Stage 10 (Multivariable analysis).
- Matrices (portfolio covariance, input–output, linearised DSGE) → Euler Stage 6 (Linear algebra).
- Difference/differential equations (Solow, Ramsey, NK dynamics) → Euler Stage 12 (ODE theory).
- Probability, expectation, variance, normal distribution → Euler Stage 17A; measure-theoretic probability and stochastic calculus behind Itô/Black–Scholes → Euler Stages 16–17B.
Where a Part needs a tool before Euler's stage has been reached, the ECO lesson states the result exactly and marks it "proved in Euler Stage n".
